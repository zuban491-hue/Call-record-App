Version 2026-11-03.1 · Effective 3 November 2026

Publication draft: replace developer/contact/URL fields before hosting.

Before enabling a feature

Make sure you understand the data it needs and have authority to use it. Obtain any required participant agreement before recording. Keep recording indicators and the Stop control visible. Never install the app covertly on someone else's device.

What is not guaranteed

Permissions do not guarantee microphone availability, both sides of a call, automatic start/stop or complete notification delivery. Permission health is access guidance, not a security certificate. A saved notification is not proof that a source message was deleted.

Keep your data private

Use a device screen lock, protect your phone from unauthorized access, review recipients before sharing, and delete unnecessary recordings. App-private storage does not protect data from a compromised operating system or a person using your unlocked device. This app does not offer its own encrypted vault.

Your choices stay separate

Agreeing to Terms does not start capture. Granting Android access does not by itself enable message history. Message-history consent is separate from call alerts and recording. You can pause features, delete local copies and revoke access.
