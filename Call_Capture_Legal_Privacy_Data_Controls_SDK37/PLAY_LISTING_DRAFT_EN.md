# Store listing draft — verify every claim against the tested release

App name: Call Capture

Short description:
Manage local microphone recordings and selected message notification history.

Full description:

Keep your saved audio and selected message notifications organized in one app.

Records lets you play, search, filter, star, annotate, share and delete local microphone recordings. Optional call channels use supported app call notifications or phone-state signals. When the app is visible and Android permits it, a connected-call signal can start microphone capture. With the app in the background, an explicit Tap to record prompt opens the app to begin capture. Live recording status and a Stop control help you stay in charge.

Messages optionally saves new notification text from selected WhatsApp and Messenger apps after you agree and enable Android notification access. A saved local copy may remain after the sender deletes the original. Search your saved notification text, pause saving, choose apps and delete copies whenever needed.

Settings includes a guided access setup, Permission health, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, recording/privacy guidance and Manage local data. Optional feature choices are explained separately. Declining optional access does not prevent you from viewing existing saved data.

Important limitations:
- Audio is microphone input. Recording both sides of calls is not guaranteed and may be unavailable on your phone.
- Call detection and automatic stopping depend on Android, the calling app and delivered signals; use Stop yourself when necessary.
- The app cannot recover old deleted chats, hidden/protected notification contents, images or voice messages. It cannot confirm whether a saved notification was actually unsent.
- Notification history is limited to text Android shares after the feature is enabled.
- Permission health reports access settings, not a security certification or recording guarantee.

Recordings and message copies stay on your device unless you deliberately share a recording. This build has no app account, advertisements, analytics SDK, automatic upload or in-app APK updater. Review the Privacy Policy for storage, retention and deletion details.

Call Capture is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp, Meta, Messenger, Telegram or Google. Use recording features only with the authority and agreement required for your situation.
